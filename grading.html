Card Bazaar Grading

We grade every card using Card Bazaar standards based on centering, edges, corners, and surface condition.

Condition tiers

  • NM (Near Mint) - Minimal wear. Crisp corners, clean edges, and a clean surface with only minor handling marks.
  • EX (Excellent) - Light wear visible on edges or corners. Surface shows small scuffs but no major defects.
  • VG (Very Good) - Noticeable wear, whitening, or scuffing. Still sleeve playable with clear text and art.

Live scan inventory

  • We avoid stock photos for premium listings to ensure transparency.
